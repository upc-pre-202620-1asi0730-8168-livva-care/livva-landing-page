About Livva

Insurance made simpler, clearer and more accessible

Livva is a digital platform designed to simplify the search, comparison and management of vehicle and health insurance alternatives.

Our goal is to reduce the complexity of choosing insurance and provide a digital experience that helps users make more informed decisions.

Transparency Clear information to compare alternatives.
Accessibility A digital experience that is easy to understand and use.
Trust Guidance to choose options according to each user's needs.

A user-centered experience

Livva brings together tools to compare alternatives, organize information and support users throughout their insurance management process.
